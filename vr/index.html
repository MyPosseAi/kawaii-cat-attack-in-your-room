This is a headset game

Open this page in the browser on your Quest 3 to play. Your real room becomes the kittens' room, and your hands are the toys.

Type or paste this address in the headset browser:

{{PLAY_URL}}

On a PC or phone there is nothing to play here, only the start card below.

Your room and hand data stay on your headset. The game records nothing and sends nothing. This test build checks once a minute for a newer version. That check sends no game data. Only the day of your last visit, how many visits you've made, and each kitten's hearts, the visit you met it on and its favourite kind of nap spot (like bed, couch or floor) are saved, in this headset's browser, so the kittens can remember you tomorrow. Feedback only goes out if you tap Send feedback, which opens a public GitHub page; anything you post there is public.

Work in progress: updated until November 14, 2026.
